# Záznam tvorby pozadí

Vytvořeno vestavěným generátorem obrázků ChatGPT. Šest samostatných prostředí ve společném pastelovém stylu STARBIT, určených jako dekorativní pozadí 2,5D plošinovky. Bez postav, UI, textu a herních plošin. Výstup 1672 × 941 px.

## forest

An inviting enchanted forest, huge rounded old trees, layered turquoise and mint woodland canopies, sunbeams, fern-covered distant rocks, small stream. No castle or houses.

## village

A cozy tiny fantasy village nestled in a forest, whimsical rounded timber cottages with mossy roofs, a waterwheel and footbridges visible in the distant scenery. No inhabitants.

## guardian

A unique ancient forest sanctuary arena background: enormous hollow tree roots frame a clearing, distant monumental broken stone arches, violet magical dusk mixed with warm golden sunlight. No portal in the illustration because gameplay supplies it.

## distant_castle

Open blue skies above a sea of clouds, floating green islands and waterfalls; a SMALL distant ivory castle with blue roofs and a gold star spire near the distant horizon. Airy spacious dream kingdom.

## near_castle

Closer approach to the SAME ivory dream castle with blue roofs and gold star spire, castle now LARGE and visibly closer, floating terraced gardens, aqueduct arches and waterfalls among pastel clouds.

## castle_court

Unique final arena backdrop at the summit of the SAME ivory blue-roofed castle: open marble courtyard beyond the gameplay plane, tall curved columns on outer edges, magnificent central palace and golden star spires, clouds beneath, warm lavender-gold sunlight. No boss, no portal.
