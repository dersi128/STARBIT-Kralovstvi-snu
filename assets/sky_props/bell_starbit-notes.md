# STARBIT bell sprite

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using Bit and the user's current game screenshot as style references. Original RGBA output is retained unchanged; Godot uses two atlas regions, so the stand remains still and the bell swings independently. Transparent pixels outside the actual component regions are not sampled.

Asset: `assets/sky_props/bell_starbit.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE production-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas for the existing Godot 2D platform game STARBIT, containing exactly TWO separate components of the SAME magical bell prop.
Input images: image 1 (mint robot Bit) is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, not a subject to reproduce. Image 2 (game screenshot) is the world/style reference only. Do not redraw the scene or robot.
Primary request: a charming small magical bell matching Bit's rounded polished cartoon forms, clean dark teal outlines, warm golden star motif, soft painted highlights, friendly children's fantasy aesthetic.
Composition: wide atlas, two equal side-by-side cells with generous transparent margins and an empty gutter. LEFT CELL: a complete freestanding wooden bell stand WITHOUT THE BELL. Two short sturdy warm brown wooden posts, rounded curved crossbeam, two small feet, tiny mint leaves near the feet, a small metal suspension hook at the top center. Empty transparent opening through the middle. RIGHT CELL: ONLY the detached golden bell, upright front view, small suspension ring at its top center, rounded bell body, broad smooth flared rim, visible round clapper below, a small turquoise band and one embossed five-point golden star on the front. Both components entirely contained in their own halves; never overlap or touch. Large clear parts suitable for separate AtlasTexture regions. Stand is about 1.5 times taller than the bell. Bell will be hung from the stand in code and swing independently.
Lighting: gentle top-left lighting, saturated cheerful gold and mint, subtle glossy highlights matching Bit. Clean opaque illustrated shapes with real alpha outside and within the stand opening.
Constraints: truly transparent background, no scenery, no ground plane, no opaque shadow rectangle, no text, no labels, no UI, no robot, no extra props, no decorative floating particles, no duplicate bells, no motion blur, no photorealism. Single consistent front-facing 2D game prop, not a concept scene.
