# Menu background

Built-in image generation was used with the existing forest and near-castle backgrounds as style references. Final delivered raster: `kingdom_adventure.png`. The title, buttons and characters remain live game UI, not part of the picture.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final 16:9 landscape background illustration for the existing STARBIT children's platform game main menu, intended for Godot. Produce a high resolution 2048x1152 landscape if possible.
Primary request: create an entirely new, beautiful adventurous menu background for STARBIT, Kingdom of Dreams.
The two input images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: image 1 is the current enchanted forest; image 2 is the current sky kingdom. Preserve their welcoming painterly cartoon 2.5D visual identity, soft rounded shapes, mint/teal greenery, warm cream stone and luminous gentle color.
Scene: an inviting grassy overlook on the border of the enchanted forest, opening onto an enormous airy valley of floating islands. A winding narrow stone path and a little rope footbridge at the lower/right edges suggest an adventure leading to a distant cream castle with blue roofs and one small golden star finial on a floating island in the upper-right third. Long slender waterfalls fall from some islands into mist. Soft clouds, turquoise atmosphere and golden morning sunlight; small white flowers in the lower corners. Layered depth, handcrafted storybook polish, cozy and exciting for children age 4–8.
Composition is CRITICAL for the existing UI: keep the central vertical zone x=33%–67% mostly quiet open pale blue sky and mist, with low contrast and minimal detail from y=5% through 90%, because the game adds its own logo at top-center and five large buttons in the middle. The scenic subjects frame the outer thirds, with a curved tree canopy in the upper-left edge, leaves in the lower-left edge, island castle and waterfalls on the right. The area x=5–28%, y=35–80% remains unobstructed enough for the existing Bit sprite, which the game overlays itself. No hard rectangular blank area, no vignette; negative space should arise naturally from sky and valley.
Output ONLY the clean background illustration, without characters, robots, NPCs, enemies, portals, text, logo, letters, UI, buttons, collectible sprites, sheet borders, watermarks or walkable game platforms. Do not copy the existing image; design a new cohesive composition that belongs to the same STARBIT world.
